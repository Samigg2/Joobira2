1. What we collect

  • Your phone number and name.
  • Your bids, wins and favourites.
  • Payment references from Chapa (we never see your PIN or bank password).
  • For winners: a photo of your ID, only to confirm who you are.

2. Why we use it

  • To sign you in with an SMS code.
  • To run auctions, find winners and deliver items.
  • To send you SMS or notifications about your bids.

3. Who we share it with

  • Chapa, to process payments.
  • Our SMS provider, to send codes and alerts.
  • We do not sell your data.

4. What others can see

  • Winners are shown by name and a hidden phone number (for example 09******75).
  • Bid history shows hidden phone numbers only.

5. How long we keep it

  • Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.
  • ID photos are deleted after the item is handed over.
