1. Who can take part

  • You must be 18 or older and have an Ethiopian mobile number.
  • One account per person. Accounts are verified by SMS code.

2. How bidding works

  • Each bid costs the price shown on the auction (the "bid price").
  • You choose a secret amount from 1.00 ETB. When the timer ends, the lowest amount that nobody else chose wins.
  • Joobiraa may extend or cancel an auction if something goes wrong; paid bids on a cancelled auction are refunded.

3. Payments

  • Bid payments are made through Chapa (Telebirr or CBE Birr).
  • Bid payments are non-refundable once the bid is placed.
  • A bid only counts after the payment is confirmed.

4. Winners

  • The winner pays the winning amount to receive the item.
  • Our team calls the winner within 24 hours to check their ID and arrange pickup or delivery.
  • If a winner does not pay or respond within 7 days, the item may be given to the next lowest unique bid.

5. Fair play

  • Bots, multiple accounts or working together to control results are not allowed.
  • Accounts that break these rules can be closed and their bids cancelled.
